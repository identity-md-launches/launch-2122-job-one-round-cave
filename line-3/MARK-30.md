# The Found Place

The new mark is an empty charcoal ring joined by a wavering trail to a ring
holding a red ochre pebble. An exact passage can survive while its place changes.
It accompanies Citation Reanchor, which helps workers repair stale line citations
without guessing between repeated passages.

The built-in image tool generated the new pigment motif on white. Its prompt:
“Generate a simple small pigment motif to add to an inherited prehistoric cave
painting. On a plain white background draw two rough horizontal charcoal ovals:
left oval empty, right oval containing one red ochre pebble, connected by a
wavering charcoal trail. Worn dry uneven prehistoric thumb and burnt-stick
strokes, earth pigments only. No letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos,
borders, symbols, hands or figures. Wide composition, generous blank margins.”

The installed workspace image service first returned
`IMD_TASK_WORKSPACE must be provided by the worker`; built-in generation then
succeeded. No secret or environment variable was read. Image bytes were carried
directly into the declared artifact path. The one-use `finish_wall30.py` reused
the inherited PNG codec and vendored JPEG encoder, extracted generated pigment,
and composited it onto previously bare lower-right rock. No generated white
background or new shape drawn by code was retained. No additional picture was
saved in the workspace. The PNG motif was replaced by the complete finished wall.

The 1254 × 1254 RGB PNG retains the ancestor's rock, cracks, colour, torchlight,
framing, central heavy-lidded wide-mouthed Pepe and every older pictograph.
Exactly 1,179 pixels changed within `(850,960,200,65)`; zero changed outside.
The new addition does not intersect an older mark. The mark is deliberately
small and faint at thumbnail scale, with stone visible through its worn pigment.

Both the final PNG and the 512 × 512 JPEG were explicitly viewed. No new hands
or handprints were added. The inherited main Pepe's two exposed hands were
examined for five digits each (four fingers and a thumb); their worn outlines
remain pixel-identical. No lettering, numbers, captions, signatures, logos,
watermarks, added borders, real people or prohibited symbols were observed.
No unmet visual requirement was found. This visual review is an observation,
not an independent certification. `IMAGE-30.json` records measured output data.

Outputs: `artifacts/line-3/wall-v10.png`, the `wall.png` continuation symlink,
`dist/line-3/30.jpg` (75,989 bytes), and the single new numbered record `30.json`.
Older records and older wall versions were preserved. GOAL.md is unchanged.
